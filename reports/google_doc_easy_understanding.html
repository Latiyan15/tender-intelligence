4. How the 0 to 100 Score is Calculated

Each tender receives a score from 0 to 100 based on a balanced formula:

  • 40% Technical Match: Does this match what Nikom engineers build? (Tier-3 DC, Cooling, Power).
  • 25% Legal & Financial Eligibility: Can Nikom legally qualify without getting disqualified by the tender committee?
  • 20% Commercial Viability: Is the contract price inside our ₹25 Lakhs to ₹25 Crores sweet spot?
  • 15% Information Completeness: Did the portal provide the full document and deadline, or is it hidden behind a CAPTCHA?
🚦 Traffic Light Decision Rules:
🟢 GO (Score ≥ 70): Strong technical fit, inside financial sweet spot, complete paperwork. Pursue bid.
🟡 CAUTION (Score 50 – 69): Equipment is related (e.g., substation transformer or DG set), but price is small or document needs manual download.
🔴 NO-GO (Score < 50 or Disqualified): Wrong domain (civil/food/medical) or demands >₹100 Cr turnover.
